VALENTINO Jewellery — Front-End UI/UX
Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.

Pages: Home, Collections, About, Contact, Bag, Checkout.
Features: luxury responsive UI, search, filters, wishlist, shared cart,
product modal, checkout UI, newsletter/contact interactions and toast notifications.
Cart and wishlist persist between pages with localStorage.

Front-end demo only: authentication, database and real payment gateway are not connected.
Images and Google Fonts are loaded online.
